Input scope: this step consumes a chosen NP-prefixing/scope tree and lexical
interpretations, not raw English. It implements operator indexing on that tree;
it does not choose movement readings or settle source-unresolved crossover and
specific-indefinite exceptions. The modal adapter is an explicitly added
integration construction, not a claim that Heim supplied one fully specified
modal/pragmatic processing algorithm. Its conservative truth-condition and
scope properties must be proved. Open relevance, prominence and proxy choices
remain named policy inputs to a functioning transition relation.
