Linde 1989. Do not change the frozen source selection or grade silently.

For Spivak, a limited non-entailment illustration may be possible; whether it
preserves a central argument remains unassessed. Historical exclusion cannot
be established by defining a model that excludes someone. A proof about a
small fragment would not warrant “Spivak formalized,” just as the existing
Derrida fragment does not formalize all of Derrida.

Every later attempt must separate supplied evidence from derived results,
disclose semantic additions, retain negative/unfinished outcomes and undergo
the proof checks in stage A. Independent semantic review and alternative-
encoding robustness are still pending. No inference about a theory's value
follows from whether a fragment is convenient to encode.
