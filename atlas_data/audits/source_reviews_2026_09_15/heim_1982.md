`dynamic/Heim_1982_DefiniteIndefiniteNPs.pdf`. Its pagination differs from the
1982 original; the edition note identifies 2011 as the retypeset edition. This

### The claimed comprehension barrier is contradicted by the code

`extras/FCS.v:73` defines `exists_ccp` by a Prop-valued existential predicate
over assignments. Its comments call that definition ill-typed and say
comprehension requires excluded middle. Fresh compilation accepts it, and
`Print Assumptions FCS.exists_ccp` reports a closed definition. A set represented
as a predicate need not have decidable membership or an enumeration algorithm.

This refutes that particular claimed obstacle. It does **not** establish that
the definition faithfully captures the whole dissertation, that every source
claim is constructive, or that an executable discourse processor has been built.
Relational nondeterminism also does not itself require selecting one canonical
witness. Claims about attitude-related impredicativity in the old attempts are
not supported by the source passages used for this proposed core.

### The donkey translation is wrong, not merely unproved

`extras/DonkeyScope.v:291` defines a formula whose exact truth conditions are:

```text
donkey_dpl(g,h) iff
  g = h and every farmer owns and beats at least one donkey.
```

The existing `unselective_binding` expresses the strong restrictor reading:
every farmer–donkey ownership pair is a beating pair. With no ownership pairs,
that reading is true. If a farmer exists, `donkey_dpl` is false in the same
setting. Thus the difference persists even before choosing between strong and
weak donkey readings: **both intended restrictor readings exclude nonowners**.

[ReviewChecks.v](ReviewChecks.v) proves
`donkey_dpl_actual_truth_conditions`, `nonowner_refutes_implemented_donkey`, and
`empty_ownership_separates_readings` directly from the current definitions.
The counterexample is stated conditionally on an empty ownership relation and
a farmer; these are ordinary model conditions. The only global dependencies
are `Entity`, `farmer`, `donkey`, `owns` and `beats`. No admitted lemma, choice
principle or inconsistent comprehension axiom is used.

## Reconciliation of all linked records

- **`extras__FCS`**: records unfaithful coverage and zero proved claims, but
  assigns `slight_modification` on the basis of proposed repairs. Adding the
  source's own domain/felicity machinery restores fidelity; it is not evidence
  that the source itself needs modification. Its direct header citation is
  Heim 1983, not the exact 1982 survey item. Reject inheritance of this outcome.
- **`extras__FCS2`**: records unfaithful coverage and `major_restructuring`.
  There are two admitted theorems and three admitted definitions. Of two proved
  lemmas, one derives `False` from the file's unrestricted class comprehension,
  and the other proves the vacuous `exists (sem : Type), True`. The Russell
  contradiction concerns the invented class axioms, not Heim or genuine
  Morse–Kelley set theory. A chosen finite-list alternative demonstrates no
  necessity to restructure Heim. Reject this outcome inference as well.
- **`extras__DonkeyScope`**: partial, `not_applicable`, seven statements/five
  proofs/two admissions. Its successful scope-ambiguity results remain valid
  evidence for those statements. They do not prove Heim's file semantics.
  Its record's characterization of the donkey translation is too favorable;
  the new exact truth-condition check supplies a correction. Signature flags
  for `Entity`, `professor`, `student` and `admires` in the existing proved
  claims are not logical choice axioms; admissions elsewhere remain a separate
  limitation. No blanket dismissal based only on assumption counts is warranted.

The historical JSON records remain intact. This draft supplies a proposed

## Proposed disposition and next implementation

Keep ID 7 `review_required`, with no accepted four-way outcome. There is no
established foundation-level obstruction and no implemented source core that
yet supports `as_is` or `slight_modification` either.

Next implement a separately named Heim core, retaining independent `Dom` and
`Sat`, condition B, atomic/composite updates and NFC. Prove domain preservation,
the novelty-based existential reading, and the universal restrictor/donkey
truth conditions, including a nonowner countermodel and a multiple-donkey test.
Model infelicity separately from false update. Explicitly scope fixed-world
semantics and accommodation. These are implementation acceptance targets, not
proofs already delivered or a precommitted paper outcome.
