# DisCoCat in Coq — final design (judge's synthesis)

Target: `/Users/graogro/Dropbox/revisiting-formal-semantics/atlas/categorical/DisCoCat.v` (create `atlas/categorical/` first). Coq 8.20.1, stdlib only (`List ZArith QArith Arith Lia Bool`), one file. Compile from the repo root: `coqc -R shallow "" -R deep "" -R extras "" -R ttr_mtt "" -R atlas "" atlas/categorical/DisCoCat.v`.

**Verdict.** Both designs agree on the calculus (`B * Z` simple types with Preller's iterator, lists as compound types, a proof-relevant `Red : Ty -> Ty -> Type`, `inhabited` for the preorder, an equational theory on derivations, soundness of the interpretation as the "well-definedness" theorem). They fork on (a) the constructor shape of the structural steps and (b) the model. On (a), Design A's `r_contr a z : red [a^z; a^(z+1)] []` / `r_exp a z : red [] [a^(z+1); a^z]` cannot state the yanking rules cast-free for a generic iterator: `η^l` at `a^z` is `r_exp a (z-1) : red [] [a^(z-1+1); a^(z-1)]`, and `z-1+1` is not convertible to `z`. Design B's four constructors indexed by `ladj a`/`radj a` avoid this (probe-verified: all four snakes typecheck with symbolic `a`). On (b), Design A's `Fin`-indexed `idx : ty -> Type` makes tensor associativity unstatable without casts, so the strict-monoidal part of the equational theory is pushed to stretch; Design B's phantom-dimension matrices with row-major Kronecker make associativity/unit laws hold on the nose (up to `mat_eq`), keep `F` free of dependent return annotations, and let the heterogeneous `deq` cover the *whole* strict compact closed theory in the MUST scope. Design B also computes faster (unary `nat` div/mod on ≤ 512 indices: 13 ms for "John likes Mary" at `N = Q^4, S = Q^2`, probe-verified). **Design B is adopted as the base.** Grafted from Design A: the Lambek single-step presentation and its equivalence theorem (STRETCH, high value); ordered basic types as a documented extension (STRETCH); an explicit `deq_cast` rule and the `F (red_cast …) = F d` lemma so casts are invisible to the semantics; the bracketing-independence corollary for the transitive sentence; the `Defined`/opacity discipline for everything on the `vm_compute` path; the "trace" lemma `ε∘η = dim`; and the observation that the intransitive reduction is Lambek's `π3 (π^r s1)` pattern. Rejected from A: generic semiring in MUST (loses `ring`; FRel is a stretch refactor), `Fin.t` indices, the equational theory restricted to concrete iterators.

**Source numbering.** The brief's "§3 pregroups, §4 compact closed + graphical calculus, §5 meaning functor, §6 worked examples" of Coecke–Sadrzadeh–Clark 2010 are, in the repository PDF, §2.2 (pp. 4–6), §3.1/§3.3 (pp. 8–14), §3.4–3.5 (pp. 14–16, Defs 3.1–3.2), §4.1–4.2 (pp. 17–23, Examples 1, 1b, 2) and §5 (pp. 24–27, Examples 3–7); §6 (p. 28) is FRel. Lambek 2008 is cited by chapter (ch. 3 p. 5, ch. 4 pp. 8–10, ch. 27 pp. 74–78, ch. 30 p. 84); Preller–Lambek 2007 §4 p. 316 (iterators and string adjoints), §3 p. 313 eq. (3.1); Sadrzadeh–Clark–Coecke 2013 §2 pp. 3–4, §4 pp. 8–9, §5 pp. 9–10, §6; Coecke–Grefenstette–Sadrzadeh 2013 Defs 4.1–4.2. All references below were checked against the PDFs.

## 1. Scope

### MUST (covers required items (1)–(4))

1. **Free pregroup on a set of basic types, as a calculus** (Lambek ch. 4 pp. 8–10, ch. 30 rules (1)–(14); Preller p. 316; Coecke 2010 §2.2). Simple types `A^(z)` with integer iterator, compound types as lists, adjoints of compound types, `Red` generated by reflexivity, transitivity, compatibility (tensor), simple contractions `a^l a → 1`, `a a^r → 1` and expansions `1 → a a^l`, `1 → a^r a`. Theorems: `Le` is a preorder; compatibility and substitutivity; `(a^l)^r = a = (a^r)^l` and the compound versions as equalities of types; `(pq)^l = q^l p^l`, `1^l = 1`; iterated adjoints; adjoint laws for compound types; contravariance; uniqueness of adjoints up to mutual reduction; the fidelity theorem that `Le` on strings is not antisymmetric (why the free pregroup is a quotient we do not form); closed derivation terms for "John likes Mary" `n (n^r s n^l) n → s`, the intransitive `n (n^r s) → s`, the adjective `n n^l n → n`, and the negative transitive sentence.
2. **Concrete compact closed model FVect_Q** (Coecke 2010 §3.1 pp. 8–9, §3.3 pp. 11–13, §6 p. 28; Sadrzadeh §2). Objects = dimensions, morphisms = matrices `nat -> nat -> Q` with phantom dimensions, tensor = row-major Kronecker, `eps`/`eta` = delta row/column. Theorems: category laws, bifunctoriality (interchange), strict associativity and unit laws of `⊗`, the four snake equations for every dimension, `ε` = inner product, trace `ε∘η = d`.
3. **Meaning map** (Coecke 2010 §3.3 p. 14, Def. 3.2 p. 16; Sadrzadeh §4; Coecke 2013 Defs 4.1–4.2). `dim : B -> nat`, `dimT` by product, `F : Red p q -> mat (dimT q) (dimT p)` by structural recursion (contraction ↦ `eps`, expansion ↦ `eta`, tensor ↦ Kronecker, transitivity ↦ composition, reflexivity ↦ identity; substitutivity ↦ tensoring with identities). Theorems: `F` is strictly monoidal on objects (`dimT`), preserves composition/tensor/identities (definitional, stated), is invariant under casts, and — the substantive one — **respects the full equational theory `deq` of strict compact closed categories on derivations** (well-definedness relative to the calculus, for all reductions); corollaries: bracketing independence and the equality of the parallel and the two sequential readings of the transitive sentence; `meaning d ws = F d (w1 ⊗ … ⊗ wn)` and its invariance under `deq`; closed forms for transitive and intransitive sentences.
4. **Worked examples by `vm_compute`** (Coecke 2010 §4.1 Examples 1, 1b; §5 Examples 3, 5): "John likes Mary" with `N = Q^4` (basis `m1, m3, f2, f4`), `S = Q^1` and `S = Q^2`, true and false instances; "John sleeps"; graded `likes = 3/4 loves + 1/4 hates`; inner products `3/4`, `1/4`, `0`; computational reflection of bracketing independence via a boolean matrix equality.

### STRETCH (in value order)
S1 negative sentence (Coecke §4.2 Example 2, eq. (7); §5 Examples 6–7) at small dimensions; S2 Frobenius algebra on `N` and the subject/object relative pronouns with the normal form `(μ_N ⊗ ι_S ⊗ ε_N)` (Sadrzadeh §2, §5, §6); S3 equivalence of `Le` with Lambek's single-step presentation (ch. 27 p. 77) — graft from A; S4 ordered basic types with parity-sensitive simple steps and Lambek's "she will come" (ch. 3 p. 5; Preller p. 317) — graft from A; S5 sound contraction-only parser producing derivations; S6 the transitive-sentence formula of Coecke §4.1 p. 18 in full generality if not finished in MUST (it is listed MUST below with a fallback); S7 scalars abstracted to a commutative semiring, FRel instance (Coecke §6).

### Deliberately out
Antisymmetry / the poset quotient (Lambek rule (5)); Preller's freeness theorem, combing lemma and decision procedure for 2-cells (so `deq` is not shown complete for planar diagrams); Buszkowski's decidability / Switching Lemma as theorems; category-theory abstractions (`Category`/`Functor` records, `FVect × P` as an object — Coecke §3.4); real scalars, norms and cosines (Def. 5.1 divides by norms; `Q` has no `sqrt`, the paper "implicitly normalises", p. 26 — exact inner products are reported); distinct spaces `V` (men) / `W` (women) of §4.1 — the functor forces `F(n) = N` (Sadrzadeh §4), so `N = span(men ∪ women)`; the Lambek-calculus side of Coecke 2013 §6.

## 2. Representation decisions (definitive)

| Source notion | Coq encoding | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| set of basic types (Coecke p. 5; Lambek ch. 3) | `Section` variable `B : Type` in the calculus and meaning modules; `Inductive basic := Bn \| Bs \| Bj \| Bsig` in `Examples` | calculus never inspects `B`; examples need a closed type for `vm_compute`; names `Bn…` avoid shadowing the ubiquitous variable `n` |
| simple type `A^(z)` (Preller p. 316; Lambek ch. 4) | `Simple := (B * Z)%type`; `ladj (b,z) := (b, Z.pred z)`; `radj (b,z) := (b, Z.succ z)` | `(a^l)^r = a` is `lia` on `Z`, an honest equality of types, no normal forms; `Z.pred/Z.succ` reduce on literals so `radj (Bn,0)` is `(Bn,1)` by `cbn` |
| compound type, free monoid (Lambek p. 9) | `Ty := list Simple`; `1 := []`; `p·q := p ++ q` | associativity/unit strict on the nose for literals (convertible), propositional (`app_assoc`, `app_nil_r`) for variables |
| `(xy)^l = y^l x^l` (Lambek p. 9; Preller p. 316) | `ladj_ty p := rev (map ladj p)`, `radj_ty` | literally Preller's `(A1^(z1)…An^(zn))^l = An^(zn−1)…A1^(z1−1)` |
| reduction as a *derivation* (Preller p. 310 "categorical imperative"; Coecke p. 13) | `Inductive Red : Ty -> Ty -> Type` with 7 constructors (D5) | `F` eliminates into matrices, so derivations must be in `Type`; four structural constructors indexed by `ladj a`/`radj a` make the snake rules cast-free |
| the order `≤` (Coecke p. 4) | `Le p q := inhabited (Red p q)` | proof-irrelevant preorder theorems; `Red` is the semantic object |
| substitutivity (Lambek (7)) | `red_subst u v d := red_tensor (red_refl u) (red_tensor d (red_refl v)) : Red (u ++ p ++ v) (u ++ q ++ v)` | `u ++ p ++ v` parses right-nested, so no cast |
| casts along list equalities | `red_cast : p = p' -> q = q' -> Red p q -> Red p' q'` by `match`, `Defined` | needed for T4/T7/T8 only; `deq_cast` makes it semantically invisible |
| equality of morphisms of the free strict compact closed category (Coecke §3.1 eqs (1)–(3), §3.3 yanking; Preller (3.1)) | `deq : forall p q p' q', Red p q -> Red p' q' -> Prop`, **heterogeneous** in the endpoints | associativity/unit laws relate derivations whose index lists are only propositionally equal; heterogeneity avoids transports; soundness is cast-free because `F d` is untyped |
| FVect object with fixed basis (Coecke p. 12) | `nat` (dimension); unit object `1` | a space with a fixed basis is `Q^d` |
| linear map / matrix | `mat m n := nat -> nat -> Q` (phantom `m` rows = codomain, `n` cols = domain); `mat_eq m n A B := forall i j, i < m -> j < n -> A i j == B i j` | dimensions live in the equality predicate and in the explicit `mmul k`/`kron m2 n2` arguments, not in the type: strict monoidal structure on the nose, no dependent casts, `vm_compute`-clean |
| composition `g ∘ f`, identity (Coecke p. 8) | `mmul k A B i j := qsum k (fun l => A i l * B l j)`; `idm i j := if Nat.eqb i j then 1 else 0` | direct |
| `f ⊗ g` (Coecke p. 9) | `kron m2 n2 A B i j := A (i / m2) (j / n2) * B (i mod m2) (j mod n2)` (`m2`,`n2` = rows/cols of `B`) | row-major Kronecker; `(A⊗B)⊗C ≡ A⊗(B⊗C)` and `A ⊗ 1_1 ≡ A` hold on the nose modulo `div`/`mod` lemmas |
| `ε : V⊗V → I`, `η : I → V⊗V` (Coecke eqs (4)–(5) p. 13; Sadrzadeh p. 3) | `eps d : mat 1 (d*d) := fun _ k => if Nat.eqb (k/d) (k mod d) then 1 else 0`; `eta d : mat (d*d) 1` its transpose | "entry 1 at `e_i ⊗ e_i`, 0 otherwise"; `V^l = V^r = V` so `ε^l = ε^r = eps` |
| vectors, `ψ : I → V` (Coecke p. 9) | columns `mat d 1`; `inner d v w := qsum d (fun i => v i 0 * w i 0)` | the source's identification |
| `Σ_{i<d}` | `Fixpoint qsum (n : nat) (f : nat -> Q)` by primitive recursion on `n` | induction on the bound; computes |
| `F(n)=N, F(s)=S, F(x^l)=F(x)^*=F(x), F(x⊗y)=F(x)⊗F(y)` (Sadrzadeh §4; Coecke 2013 Def 4.1) | `dimS (b,_) := dim b`; `dimT := fold_right (fun a d => (dimS a * d)%nat) 1%nat` | direct; iterator ignored = `F(x^l) = F(x)` definitional |
| `F` on reductions (Coecke Def 3.2; Sadrzadeh §4) | `Fixpoint F {p q} (d : Red p q) : mat (dimT q) (dimT p)` (D13) | no `return` clause needed (phantom) |
| meaning of a string (Coecke Def 3.2; Coecke 2013 Def 4.2) | `tensor_words : list (Ty * (nat -> nat -> Q)) -> mat _ 1` as a right fold of `kron (dimT rest) 1`; `meaning d ws := mmul (dimT p) (F d) (tensor_words ws)` | right fold aligns with `dimT` and right-nested `red_tensor` |
| scalars `R` | `Q` with `Qeq`; `ring` on `Q` | every number in the examples is rational; `R` does not compute |

**Assumptions.** Constructive and axiom-free (no functional extensionality: setoid equalities; no proof irrelevance: `Red` in `Type`; no JMeq: heterogeneous `deq` + phantom dims). Finite dimension with a fixed basis is the source's own assumption. Basic types form a *set* in MUST scope (Coecke's examples use none; Lambek's poset is S4). `Q` equality decidable (`Qeq_bool`), used only for computed checks. `dim b = 0` allowed: all model theorems are bounded-quantified; `x / 0 = 0`, `x mod 0 = x` are definitional in 8.20, and `Nat.mod_upper_bound`/`Nat.div_add_l` need `d <> 0`, discharged by the vacuity of `i < m*0`.

## 3. Definitions

Five modules in one file: `Pregroup` (Section over `B`), `QSum`, `FVect`, `Meaning` (Section over `B`, `dim`), `Examples`; `Frobenius` and `Lambek` (stretch) after.

**Module `Pregroup`**

- D1 `Simple := (B * Z)%type`; `ladj`, `radj`. — Preller p. 316; Lambek ch. 4 p. 8.
- D2 `Ty := list Simple`; `[]`, `++`. — Lambek ch. 4 p. 9.
- D3 `ladj_ty p := rev (map ladj p)`; `radj_ty`. — Preller p. 316; Lambek p. 9 `(xy)^l = y^l x^l, 1^l = 1`.
- D4 `Le p q : Prop := inhabited (Red p q)`. — Coecke §2.2 p. 4.
- D5 ```
Inductive Red : Ty -> Ty -> Type :=
| red_refl   p : Red p p
| red_trans  p q r : Red p q -> Red q r -> Red p r
| red_cup_l  a : Red [ladj a; a] []          (* a^l a ≤ 1 *)
| red_cup_r  a : Red [a; radj a] []          (* a a^r ≤ 1 *)
| red_cap_l  a : Red [] [a; ladj a]          (* 1 ≤ a a^l *)
| red_cap_r  a : Red [] [radj a; a]          (* 1 ≤ a^r a *)
| red_tensor p q p' q' : Red p p' -> Red q q' -> Red (p ++ q) (p' ++ q').
```
  After `End` of the section, re-declare `Arguments red_trans {B p q r}`, `Arguments red_tensor {B p q p' q'}`, `Arguments red_refl {B}` etc. (implicit declarations made inside a section do not survive `End`; probe-verified). — Lambek ch. 30 (3),(4),(6),(8),(9); Coecke §2.2 p. 5 and §3.3 p. 13 (`ε^l = [p^l p ≤ 1]`, `ε^r = [p p^r ≤ 1]`, `η^l = [1 ≤ p p^l]`, `η^r = [1 ≤ p^r p]`).
- D6 `red_cast (e1 : p = p') (e2 : q = q') (d : Red p q) : Red p' q'` by `match e1, e2 with eq_refl, eq_refl => d end`, `Defined`.
- D7 `red_subst u v (d : Red p q) : Red (u ++ p ++ v) (u ++ q ++ v)` (cast-free). — Lambek ch. 27 p. 74, rule (7).
- D8 Named derivations by structural recursion on `p` (`Definition`s, `Defined`): `cup_l_ty p : Red (ladj_ty p ++ p) []`, `cup_r_ty p : Red (p ++ radj_ty p) []`, `cap_l_ty p : Red [] (p ++ ladj_ty p)`, `cap_r_ty p : Red [] (radj_ty p ++ p)`. — Lambek ch. 4 p. 9, ch. 27 (2); Preller (3.3).
- D9 ```
Inductive deq : forall p q p' q', Red p q -> Red p' q' -> Prop :=
  deq_refl | deq_sym | deq_trans
| deq_cong_trans : deq d1 d1' -> deq d2 d2' -> deq (red_trans d1 d2) (red_trans d1' d2')
| deq_cong_tensor : deq d1 d1' -> deq d2 d2' -> deq (red_tensor d1 d2) (red_tensor d1' d2')
| deq_assoc   : deq (red_trans (red_trans d1 d2) d3) (red_trans d1 (red_trans d2 d3))
| deq_unit_l  : deq (red_trans (red_refl p) d) d      | deq_unit_r : deq (red_trans d (red_refl q)) d
| deq_tensor_assoc : deq (red_tensor (red_tensor d1 d2) d3) (red_tensor d1 (red_tensor d2 d3))
| deq_tensor_unit_l : deq (red_tensor (red_refl []) d) d
| deq_tensor_unit_r : deq (red_tensor d (red_refl [])) d
| deq_tensor_refl : deq (red_tensor (red_refl p) (red_refl q)) (red_refl (p ++ q))
| deq_interchange : deq (red_trans (red_tensor f1 f2) (red_tensor g1 g2)) (red_tensor (red_trans f1 g1) (red_trans f2 g2))
| deq_cast : deq (red_cast e1 e2 d) d
| deq_snake_1 a : deq (red_trans (red_tensor (red_cap_l a) (red_refl [a])) (red_tensor (red_refl [a]) (red_cup_l a))) (red_refl [a])
| deq_snake_2 a : deq (red_trans (red_tensor (red_refl [a]) (red_cap_r a)) (red_tensor (red_cup_r a) (red_refl [a]))) (red_refl [a])
| deq_snake_3 a : deq (red_trans (red_tensor (red_refl [ladj a]) (red_cap_l a)) (red_tensor (red_cup_l a) (red_refl [ladj a]))) (red_refl [ladj a])
| deq_snake_4 a : deq (red_trans (red_tensor (red_cap_r a) (red_refl [radj a])) (red_tensor (red_refl [radj a]) (red_cup_r a))) (red_refl [radj a]).
```
  All four snakes typecheck with symbolic `a` (probe-verified). — Coecke §3.1 pp. 8–9 (associativity, identities, eqs (1)–(3)), §3.3 p. 11 (the four yanking equations); Preller (3.1) p. 313, Prop. 1.
- D10 (S3) `Inductive step : Ty -> Ty -> Prop` (generalized contraction `u ++ [a; radj a] ++ v ⇒ u ++ v`, `u ++ [ladj a; a] ++ v ⇒ u ++ v`; generalized expansion, the converses); `le_step := clos_refl_trans _ step`. — Lambek ch. 27 p. 77 (proof of the Switching Lemma, the three step forms with a discrete basic order).
- D11 (S4) constructor `red_basic a b z : ble_z z a b -> Red [(a,z)] [(b,z)]` with `ble_z z a b := if Z.even z then ble a b else ble b a`. — Lambek ch. 27 p. 77 "simple step"; Preller p. 317 (`s^(z)`).

**Module `Examples` types** (declared early, in `Pregroup`'s wake, so derivations can be checked before the model exists)

- D12 `basic := Bn | Bs | Bj | Bsig`; `n := (Bn,0%Z)`, `s := (Bs,0%Z)`, `j`, `sg`; `tv := [radj n; s; ladj n]`, `iv := [radj n; s]`, `adj := [n; ladj n]`, `does := [radj n; s; ladj j; sg]`, `not := [radj sg; j; ladj j; sg]`, `like := [radj sg; j; ladj n]`. — Coecke §2.2 pp. 5–6; Coecke 2013 Table (adjective); Lambek ch. 3 p. 5 (`π3 (π^r s1 …)` intransitive pattern).
- D13 Derivation terms (`Definition`, closed, cast-free by conversion of `++` on literals):
  `tv_red : Red [n; radj n; s; ladj n; n] [s] := red_tensor (red_cup_r n) (red_tensor (red_refl [s]) (red_cup_l n))` (the morphism `ε^r_n ⊗ 1_s ⊗ ε^l_n`, Coecke p. 14);
  `tv_red_subj` := contract `n n^r` first then `n^l n` (two-layer `red_trans`), `tv_red_obj` := object first (the step-by-step reduction of Coecke p. 6);
  `tv_red_alt := red_tensor (red_tensor (red_cup_r n) (red_refl [s])) (red_cup_l n)` (other bracketing);
  `iv_red : Red [n; radj n; s] [s]`; `adj_red : Red [n; ladj n; n] [n] := red_tensor (red_refl [n]) (red_cup_l n)`;
  `neg_red : Red (n :: does ++ not ++ like ++ [n]) [s]` as `red_trans` of the layer `ε^r_n ⊗ 1_s ⊗ 1_{j^l} ⊗ ε^r_σ ⊗ 1_j ⊗ 1_{j^l} ⊗ ε^r_σ ⊗ 1_j ⊗ ε^l_n` and the layer `1_s ⊗ ε^l_j ⊗ ε^l_j` (Coecke §2.2 p. 6, §4.2 p. 21).

**Module `Meaning`** (Section over `B`, `dim : B -> nat`)

- D20 `dimS`, `dimT`. — Sadrzadeh §4; Coecke 2013 Def 4.1.
- D21 ```
Fixpoint F {p q} (d : Red p q) : mat (dimT q) (dimT p) :=
  match d with
  | red_refl _ => idm
  | @red_trans _ q _ d1 d2 => mmul (dimT q) (F d2) (F d1)
  | red_cup_l a | red_cup_r a => eps (dimS a)
  | red_cap_l a | red_cap_r a => eta (dimS a)
  | @red_tensor _ q _ q' d1 d2 => kron (dimT q') (dimT q) (F d1) (F d2)
  end.
```
  (probe-verified as written, no `return` clause). — Coecke Def 3.2 p. 16 and p. 14; Sadrzadeh §4 (`F(ε^r_n ⊗ 1_s ⊗ ε^l_n) = ε_N ⊗ 1_S ⊗ ε_N`); Coecke 2013 Def 4.2.
- D22 `tensor_words (ws : list (Ty * (nat -> nat -> Q))) : mat (dimT (concat (map fst ws))) 1 := fold_right (fun '(t,v) acc => kron (dimT (types of the rest)) 1 v acc) (fun _ _ => 1) ws` — implement as a recursive function that carries the dimension of the tail; `meaning d ws := mmul (dimT p) (F d) (tensor_words ws)`. **The `m2` argument of `kron` must be the full row dimension of the tail (`dimT (rest)`), not the dimension of the next word** (probe bug found and fixed). — Coecke Def 3.2 `f(w1 ⊗ … ⊗ wn)`; Coecke 2013 Def 4.2.

**Module `Examples`**

- D23 `dim1 : basic -> nat` (`Bn ↦ 4, Bs ↦ 1, Bj ↦ 1, Bsig ↦ 4`) and `dim2` (`Bs ↦ 2, Bj ↦ 2`); basis `m1 := e 4 0`, `m3 := e 4 1`, `f2 := e 4 2`, `f4 := e 4 3`; `john := m3`, `mary := f4`; `likes_rel : nat -> nat -> bool`; `likes2 : mat (4*2*4) 1 := fun r _ => let i := r/8, t := (r/4) mod 2, k := r mod 4 in if Nat.eqb t (if likes_rel i k then 1 else 0) then 1 else 0` (row-major `i*8 + t*4 + k`), `likes1` for the 1-dim `S` (`likes_ij = 1`, origin = false). — Coecke §4.1 Example 1 p. 19 ("John is m3, Mary is f4", `likes_ij = 1 iff m_i likes f_j`), 1b p. 20.

## 5. Expected encoding artifacts (each marked `(* ARTIFACT (class): … *)` at its site, listed in the file header)

- **(i) Strict positivity.** Not triggered: `Red`, `deq`, `step` are first-order indexed inductives with no function-typed constructor arguments. The Frobenius "spider" normal forms are *not* represented as arbitrary-arity nodes. State explicitly that no positivity workaround was needed.
- **(ii) Universe / type–value collapse.** (a) `Red : Ty -> Ty -> Type` must live in `Type` for `F` to eliminate into matrices; `Le := inhabited ∘ Red` is its `Prop` shadow — the brief's "reduction relation" is the pair. (b) FVect objects are `nat` values and `mat m n` is a phantom alias of `nat -> nat -> Q`: dimension mismatches are not type errors; correctness is carried by `mat_eq m n` and the explicit `mmul k`/`kron m2 n2` arguments; ill-shaped compositions compute junk, never fail to typecheck. (c) Pregroup types are lists (values); "objects of `FVect × P`" are never a Coq type — the pair `(F d, d)` is the morphism. (d) `F(x^l) = F(x)` is *definitional* (`dimS` ignores the iterator) and so cannot be a non-trivial theorem. (e) Distinct `V`/`W` of Coecke §4.1 collapse to `N`. Tags at D4/D5, D15, D20, D21, D23.
- **(iii) Computational opacity.** (a) `Qeq` is not Leibniz: `vm_compute` yields unreduced fractions, so computed statements go through `Qred`/`entries` or `Qeq_bool`; all algebraic theorems are setoid equalities and rewriting needs the `Proper` instances of T17. (b) Vectors are functions with junk outside `[0,dim)`. (c) Everything on the `vm_compute` path is a transparent `Definition`/`Fixpoint`/`Defined` with no proof terms: derivations D13, `red_cast`, `F`, `tensor_words`, lexicon, `qsum`, `mat_eqb`, `entries`. `red_cast` applied to the `Qed`-opaque `app_assoc` blocks `vm_compute` — T4/T7/T8 derivations are never computed; if one must be, use a local transparent `app_assoc'` (`Defined`). (d) Unary `nat` `div`/`mod` in `kron`: ≈ 10³ columns instant, 10⁴ seconds, 10⁶ infeasible (bounds T38). Tags at D6, D13, D19, T38.
- **(iv) Missing native structure.** No category/functor abstraction: every law is a standalone theorem about the concrete model (T18–T21, T39) and about `F` (T26–T28); the "functor" is a `Fixpoint`. No dependent fixed-size matrices (`Vector.t`/`Fin.t` rejected: they force casts for `(m*n)*k = m*(n*k)` and dependent `return` clauses). No bigops: `qsum` with its own kit. No reals (norms/cosines dropped, T36 states exact inner products). No quotient types: `deq` is an explicit equational theory; the quotient category is not formed and completeness for planar diagrams (Preller Thm 1) is not proved. No planar-graph transitions. Basic order absent in MUST (hook D11). Tags at the `FVect` header, D9, D14, T36.
- **(v) Decidability gap.** Grammaticality (`Le`) is decidable in the literature (Coecke p. 5; Buszkowski) but only a sound checker (T42) is provided; derivations are hand-written. `deq` is decidable in the source (Preller §1) but not here. `mat_eq` at fixed dims is decided by `mat_eqb` (T24), so semantic consequences of `deq` are reflected computationally (T37) even where the syntactic equality is not decided. Antisymmetry fails on strings (T9); the poset quotient is not constructed; all theorems are at the preorder level, which is where the meaning map lives. Tags at D4, T9, T24, T42.

## 6. Pitfalls (probe-verified where marked ✓)

1. **Scopes.** ✓ `Local Open Scope Q_scope` inside a `Section` *leaks past `End`* in 8.20: after it, bare numerals and `*` in later `nat` definitions parse as `Q` (`dim`, `dimT`, `seq 0 d`, `v i 0` all broke in the probe). Define `dimT` as `fold_right (fun a d => (dimS a * d)%nat) 1%nat`, annotate every `nat` literal/operation `%nat` in `Q`-scoped regions, use `Nat.eqb` never `=?`, and iterators `%Z`. Prefer opening `Q_scope` only inside `QSum`/`FVect`/`Examples` bodies and closing it explicitly (`Close Scope Q_scope`).
2. **Implicit arguments and sections.** ✓ `Arguments red_tensor {p q p' q'}` declared inside the section is lost at `End`; re-declare after the section with `B` included (`{B p q p' q'}`), or example terms fail with "expected type `Ty ?B`".
3. **`kron` dimension arguments.** ✓ `kron m2 n2 A B` takes the *full* row/column dimensions of the right factor. In `tensor_words`, the right factor is the whole tail (`dimT rest`), not the next word — using the next word's dimension silently gives a wrong (all-zero) meaning. Sanity-check every `kron` with a computed example before proving anything.
4. **`Nat.Div0` names (8.20).** ✓ See T16. `Nat.div_add_l` and `Nat.mod_upper_bound` carry `b <> 0`; the `Div0.*` family is unconditional. `lia` cannot handle `/`/`mod` with variable divisors and `nia` times out on three-index splits — pre-prove `div_add_small`/`mod_add_small` and rewrite with them; use `ring` (on `nat`) to normalise `a*(n*k) + (b*k+c)` to `(a*n+b)*k + c` before applying them.
5. **`qsum` by primitive recursion on the bound**, not `fold_right`/`seq`: induction on the bound is direct; `qsum_split` follows from `qsum_app` with `S m * n = n + m*n` definitional. Prove `qsum_swap` before `mmul_assoc`.
6. **`Qeq` setoid.** Rewriting under `qsum`/`mmul` fails without `Proper` instances (`Add Parametric Morphism` for `qsum` w.r.t. pointwise `Qeq`, then `mmul`, `kron`); `ring` proves `==` goals via the `QArith` ring instance; `lia`/`nia` do not know `Q`. Computed statements use `Qred`/`entries` (`Qred (4#4) = 1#1`).
7. **Indexed inductive `Red` with list indices.** Example derivations must be given with `exact`/`refine` or as `Definition` terms — `apply red_tensor` cannot unify `[n; n^r; s; n^l; n]` with `?p ++ ?q`. Never `rewrite` in the indices of `Red`; state cast lemmas with fully general endpoints so `destruct` works; never define `deq` homogeneously; do not `inversion` on `Red` (state facts on derivations instead).
8. **`red_tensor` and `app_assoc`.** `u ++ p ++ v` is right-nested (no cast), but `ladj_ty (a::p) ++ (a::p) = (ladj_ty p ++ [ladj a]) ++ a :: p` needs `app_assoc` — give the instance explicitly (`app_assoc (ladj_ty p) [ladj a] (a::p)`); unification fails when one side is only convertible after unfolding `[x] ++ l`. Keep casts out of `F` (T27 + `deq_cast`).
9. **`Z` iterators.** ✓ `simpl` does not unfold `ladj`/`radj` on a pair literal; write `unfold radj, ladj; simpl; f_equal; lia`. `Z.pred`/`Z.succ` reduce on literals so `radj n` is `(Bn, 1)` by `cbn` in example types.
10. **`vm_compute` hygiene.** ✓ No `Qed` anywhere in the computation chain (a `Qed` cast silently leaves the goal unreduced). Never `Eval vm_compute` a bare `mat` (prints a closure): go through `entries`/`mat_eqb`. `Time` every example; keep MUST examples ≤ 512 columns; the negative sentence only at all-dims-2 (8192 columns).
11. **`dimT [a] = dimS a * 1`, not `dimS a`.** T28's snake cases and `F_tv` need `Nat.mul_1_r` (or state the model snakes with `idm` at `d*1`).
12. **Bracketing of tensors.** `F tv_red` has domain `(4*(4*1)) * ((2*1) * (4*(4*1)))`-shaped expressions while `tensor_words` has `4 * (32 * (4 * 1))`; `mmul k` needs only numeric equality (`lia`/`qsum_dim`), and `kron_assoc` (T20) is why entries agree. Do not "fix" this with casts.
13. **Two sentence-space dimensions.** `dim` is a `Section` variable of `Meaning`; `Examples` instantiates it twice (`dim1`, `dim2`) without duplicating `F`. For `neg_red` set `dim Bsig = dim Bn`, `dim Bj = dim Bs`, otherwise `does_vec`'s `η_V` wire has the wrong dimension.
14. **Zero dimensions.** `x / 0 = 0`, `x mod 0 = x`; T19–T21 remain provable without `0 < d` because `i < m*0` is absurd, but `qsum_delta` needs `j < n` — always carry the range hypotheses from `mat_eq`. Split `d = 0` before using `Nat.div_add_l`.
15. **Names.** `Import ListNotations`; do not `Import Vector`; avoid `F` as both a section variable and a definition; `mu`/`delta`/`eps`/`eta`/`swap` are free (nothing from `Reals` is imported); basic types `Bn | Bs | Bj | Bsig` (never `n | s`).
16. **Fidelity traps to comment, not fix.** `ε^l`/`ε^r` superscripts differ between Coecke 2010 p. 14 (`ε^r_n ⊗ 1_s ⊗ ε^l_n`) and Sadrzadeh 2013 §4 (`ε^r_n ⊗ 1_s ⊗ ε^r_n`, a typo there); constructors follow Coecke §3.3, and in the model both are `eps`. `V`(men)/`W`(women) vs functorial `N`. Coecke's "§6" in the brief is FRel, not the examples.
