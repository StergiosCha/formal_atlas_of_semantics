10 October 2026 | For Robin Cooper and Rasmus Blanck

The earlier packet remains an accurate record of its bounded internal
preservation and soundness results. It must not be used as a certificate
that Montague's original PTQ paper has been fully formalized.

Direct consultation of the primary text confirms source deviations already
recorded in the repository's older audit:

- PTQ's IV/CN interpretation type returns t; the old deep file inserted
  an additional intension in the result type.
- PTQ's necessity ranges over all world/time pairs, not just worlds at
  the current time.
- PTQ's English translation was not implemented as a recursive compiler
  in that old file, despite its header suggesting the full pipeline.

The newer preservation theorem correctly preserves those old choices. It
does not repair them. Our earlier conversational description of fixed-time
necessity as PTQ's own choice was incorrect and is withdrawn.

The new `deep/montague_ptq/` branch reconstructs the source definitions with
the correct types, model conditions and all typed T1-T17 compiler cases.
It proves agreement with an independent analysis-tree interpretation and
supplies a model satisfying all nine meaning-postulate families. It now also
proves named-to-intrinsic interpretation preservation and the equivalence
between the nine actual IL formula families and semantic admissibility.

five nonreferential witnesses are checked. See `FINISHING_REPORT.md` for the
443-statement checkpoint. All 26 original candidate analysis/formula pairs and
the exact editorial fresh-name procedure now have correspondence proofs.

strings are ungenerated by the literal rules. The blanket positive generation
claim is refuted. A separately labelled S4 present-inflection amendment now
restores all 20 registered strings with the 26 existing analysis/formula
certificates. General safety proofs establish that only verb forms change,
realization failure is preserved, and the original rendering policy is
unchanged. The original 17 Coq modules are untouched. See
`SURFACE_AMENDMENT.md`; exhaustive reading classification is still not claimed.
