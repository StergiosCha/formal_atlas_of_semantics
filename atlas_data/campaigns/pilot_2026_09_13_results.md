All three compile and their theorem declarations are closed under the global
context. The source-level determination for each remains **unassessed**, and
the atlas marks the work as a pilot (F3). The intrinsic grades remain P1/P2/P3.
The probes flag 3 of these 17 declarations as trivial (Grice 1, Tarski 2),
with no vacuity flags or bailouts. These are diagnostic results, not a claim
that every remaining theorem is scientifically substantive.

The substantive contrasts are in the kinds of commitments exposed, not the
raw theorem totals. Grice's relevant reasoning needs context/belief structure
that the selected prose conditions do not turn into an algorithm. This is a
source/encoding boundary, not evidence that an arbitrary chosen algorithm
was required by the theory. Tarski's outline supports a precise recursive
construction once a language is fixed. Davidson's explicit event argument
directly supports the tested inference and shared-witness countermodel.

No source claim has been reported as refuted. The countermodels delimit
inferences the sources do not license. No extra premises have been hidden
as axioms; all abstract parameters and assumptions occur in the statements.
Definition checks, elementary consequences and metatheorems are not empirical
confirmation, and the probe sidecars must accompany any use of the counts.

This small topic-restricted, single-researcher pilot cannot estimate a
formalizability rate or establish a formality gradient. Independent semantic
review and broader preregistered sampling are still needed before such claims.
