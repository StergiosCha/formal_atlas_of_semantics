Limits: strategy comparison is conditional mathematical semantics, not an
empirical ranking or automated proof of conversational appropriateness.

recency threshold, complete repair search, global preference, or deployment is
authorized by this comparison. The no-repair/local/global selector applies to a
declared fresh-description repair request, not to every arbitrary utterance.
