For each source: inventory ontology, definitions, claims, and glosses with
printed page references before coding. Attempt one central inferential
mechanism, its motivating instance, and a boundary/non-entailment test.
Scope cap: one Coq module per source, not a whole-book or whole-theory claim.
Log a missing specification instead of replacing the source's mechanism
silently. Do not replace a difficult selected source with an easier one.

Report separately: source content; explicit encoding choices; proved/refuted
claims; extra assumptions; empirical or not-statable remainder; unfinished
engineering. Check compilation and Print Assumptions. A proof by unfolding
is a definition check, not independent support for an empirical prediction.

Theory-level outcomes are withheld for incomplete fragments. No revised
P grade, resistance-rate estimate, or claim of a formality/outcome gradient
may be inferred from these three purposively restricted cases. Store results
in a separate file; do not edit this protocol retrospectively.
